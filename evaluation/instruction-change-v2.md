## Priority and category clarifications (added in Module 9)
- eSIM or SIM problems that leave the customer with no working SIM are P2. Device faults that still allow service are P3.
- Call drops or slow data that still allow some service are P3, even when a known incident exists.
- Abusive or sarcastic wording doesn't change the category. Classify by the underlying issue, and add the abusive_language flag if needed.
- If you can't tell which service the customer means, use UNCLASSIFIED and P3, and ask one clarifying question.
