# Contoso Telecom - Roaming Support Guide

Document ID: KB-ROA-040
Classification: Synthetic training content for the Microsoft Foundry agent lab.

## No network or no data while abroad
1. Confirm roaming is enabled on the account.
2. Ask the customer to switch on "Data roaming" in the phone settings.
3. Ask the customer to select a partner network manually (Settings > Mobile network > Network operators).
4. Restart the phone after landing.
5. If the problem continues, the Roaming Desk can refresh the customer's roaming profile remotely.

## Priority guidance
- A single consumer with no service abroad: P2.
- A business customer with several lines without service abroad: P1. The Roaming Desk contacts the business
  account manager as soon as the ticket is raised.

## Roaming bundles
- Customers can buy roaming day passes in the Contoso Telecom app (Plans > Roaming). Changes take effect within 15 minutes.
