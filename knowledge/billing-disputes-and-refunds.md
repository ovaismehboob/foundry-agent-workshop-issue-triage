# Contoso Telecom - Billing Disputes, Refunds and Payment Suspensions

Document ID: KB-BIL-030
Classification: Synthetic training content for the Microsoft Foundry agent lab.

## Duplicate or incorrect charges
- Check the Known Incidents Bulletin first. Some duplicate charges are known incidents with automatic refunds.
- For other disputes, Billing Operations reviews the charge within 3 business days.
- Customers do not need to pay a disputed amount while it is under review.
- Never promise a refund amount or date unless the Known Incidents Bulletin or Billing Operations has confirmed it.

## Bundle charged but not renewed
- Treat as a billing dispute (P3). Billing Operations checks the renewal log and either applies the bundle or refunds the charge.

## Line suspended after payment
- Payments made through bank transfer can take up to 2 hours to be applied. Card and app payments are normally applied immediately.
- If a customer has paid and the line is still suspended, this is a P2 issue for Billing Operations:
  the customer has lost service. Billing Operations can restore service manually after confirming the payment.

## Invoice requests
- Customers can download invoices from the Contoso Telecom app (Account > Bills). This is an information request (P4)
  unless it is part of a bigger issue.
