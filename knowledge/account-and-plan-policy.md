# Contoso Telecom - Account, Plan Change and Ownership Policy

Document ID: KB-ACC-050
Classification: Synthetic training content for the Microsoft Foundry agent lab.

## Plan or bundle changes
- Prepaid customers can upgrade in the Contoso Telecom app (Plans > Change plan). Upgrades apply immediately;
  downgrades apply at the next renewal date.
- Plan change requests are P4 (information or change request) unless the customer has lost service.

## Transfer of number ownership
- Both the current owner and the new owner must visit a Contoso Telecom store together with valid identity documents.
- Ownership cannot be transferred through the app, by email, or by phone.
- Priority guidance: P4.
