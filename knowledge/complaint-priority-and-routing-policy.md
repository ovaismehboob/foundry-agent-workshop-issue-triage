# Contoso Telecom - Complaint Handling, Priority and Routing Policy

Document ID: KB-POL-060
Classification: Synthetic training content for the Microsoft Foundry agent lab.

## Priority definitions
| Priority | Name | Definition | First response |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Critical | Complete loss of service for a business customer or for multiple lines, or impact on emergency calling. | 1 hour |
| P2 | High | Complete loss of a key service for a single consumer, service suspended, or a third repeat complaint. | 4 hours |
| P3 | Medium | Partial degradation, billing disputes, device faults, or an issue with a known workaround. | 24 hours |
| P4 | Low | Information requests, plan changes, feedback, or out-of-scope messages. | 72 hours |

## Routing
| Category | Team |
|---|---|
| Network & Connectivity | Network Operations (P1: Network Operations Centre, Major Incident Desk) |
| Device, SIM & Services | Device & SIM Support |
| Billing & Payments | Billing Operations |
| Roaming | Roaming Desk |
| Account & Plan Changes | Account Services |
| Service Complaint | Customer Relations |
| Unclassified / Needs Information | Frontline Support |

## Complaint handling
- A customer who has complained two or more times in the last 90 days about an unresolved issue is a repeat complainant.
  Their next complaint is at least P2 and is owned by Customer Relations.
- Staff must stay professional even when a customer uses abusive language. Abusive language does not change the
  priority on its own, but threats of violence must be reported to a team leader.
- Acknowledge every complaint within the first-response time and give the customer a ticket reference.
